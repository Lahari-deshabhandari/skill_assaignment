Uttara Kannada is the fifth largest district in the Indian state of Karnataka. 
It is bordered by the state of Goa and Belagavi districts to the north, Dharwad and Haveri Districts to the east, Shivamogga and Udupi Districts to the south, and the Laccadive Sea to the west. 
Karwar is the district headquarters while Sirsi is the major commercial center in the district. 
The district's agroclimatic divisions include the coastal plain which consists of Karwar, Ankola, Kumta, Honnavar, the Bhatkal taluks and Malenadu, consisting of Sirsi, Siddapur, Yellapur, Haliyal, Dandeli, Joida and the Mundgod taluks.
